# 📜 Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want the Birthday Card project to be a welcoming and respectful place for everyone interested in contributing.

We welcome contributions from developers of all experience levels.

---

## ✅ Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

* Be respectful and professional.
* Welcome different ideas and perspectives.
* Give constructive feedback.
* Focus on improving the project.
* Respect other contributors.
* Give appropriate credit for other people's work.
* Communicate clearly and respectfully.

---

## ❌ Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment
* Personal attacks
* Discrimination
* Insults
* Trolling
* Threats
* Sharing private information without permission
* Deliberately disrupting the project
* Claiming another contributor's work as your own

---

## 💬 Discussions

Different opinions are welcome.

However, disagreements should remain focused on the project and should not become personal.

---

## 🚨 Reporting Problems

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer through GitHub.

The maintainer may take whatever action is considered appropriate to protect the project community.
